# Performance, lighting, power

## Performance

- **DPI stages** — up to five, each with its own X and Y (100–30000 depending on the model). The stages are switched with the DPI buttons behind the wheel; the active one is lit in its colour. They belong to the profile.
- **Polling rate** — 125, 500 or 1000 Hz (more on models that support it).

## Lighting

Zones of the Naga Pro: **scroll wheel**, **logo**, **side panel**. Effects: static, spectrum, wave, breathing (one colour, two colours, random), reactive, and for the side panel custom colours of its three LEDs. **Brightness** per zone; **Same for all zones** applies one setting everywhere.

The mouse does not report the current effect, so the page shows what was last set in open-rzr.

On Linux, while **Polychromatic** runs, it manages the lighting and the page is locked (see [Linux](linux.md)).

## Power

- **Sleep after** — how long a wireless mouse waits without movement before it sleeps.
- **Low power mode** — below this battery level the lighting is dimmed.
- The battery level and charging are shown at the top right.
